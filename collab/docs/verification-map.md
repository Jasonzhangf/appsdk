runtime behavior. Source consumers are under `appserver_two_tui_integration`,
including `name_runtime_cli`, `name_adjudication`, named send/recv and handoff cases.
The `waiting_user` parser has fixture evidence; a real selection UI sample remains
separate from that evidence.
